# YOLO

Top-down tank action in a maze of enemy bases. Destroy every base's reactors before its defenders wear you down. Runs in any modern browser on desktop, phone, or tablet, with keyboard and mouse, gamepad, or touch.

**Play:** [GitHub Pages](https://plinkbit.github.io/yolo/) · itch.io (coming soon)

## Controls

| Action           | Keyboard + mouse     | Gamepad             | Touch                         |
|------------------|----------------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Drive            | WASD or arrow keys   | Left stick / D-pad  | Left thumb                    |
| Aim              | Mouse                | Right stick         | Right thumb                   |
| Fire             | Left click           | RT or RB            | Right thumb                   |
| Boost            | Shift                | LB                  | Push left stick past its ring |
| Demolition shell | Right click or Space | LT                  | SHELL button                  |
| Pause            | Escape or P          | Start               | II button                     |
| Music on/off     | M                    | Y                   | Tap the music line            |
| Music volume     | Left / Right on the title or pause screen | D-pad left / right, same screens | Tap the arrows |
| How to Play      | H on the title screen | B                  | HOW TO PLAY button            |
| High scores      | T on the title screen | X                  | HIGH SCORES button            |

Boost burns fuel. Music volume runs from 0 (off) to 5.

## Modes and scores

The game is played sector by sector; each one is bigger and better defended than the last, and from sector 12 on the enemy keeps getting faster while everything scores more.

Choose 3 lives or **YOLO** on the title screen. A YOLO run has one tank, no spares and no bonus tanks. There are two top-10 tables: one for every run, one for YOLO runs only. A YOLO run counts on both.
